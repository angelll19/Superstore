
# 📊 EMEA Profitability Analysis

### Identifying the Root Causes of Profit Loss and Evaluating Improvement Strategies

---

## Overview

This project investigates the unusually low profitability of the EMEA market through a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, discount strategies, shipping costs, and country-level contributions.

Using business intelligence and exploratory analysis techniques, the study identifies Turkey as the primary driver of EMEA's poor financial performance and evaluates potential strategies to improve profitability through scenario modeling.

---

## Business Problem

Despite generating substantial sales, the EMEA market reported the lowest profit margin compared to other regions.

The objective of this analysis was to answer the following questions:

* Why is EMEA underperforming?
* Which countries contribute most to the issue?
* Are discount strategies reducing profitability?
* How do shipping costs impact profit?
* What actions could improve overall market performance?

---

## Key Insights

### 🌍 EMEA Underperformed Relative to Other Markets

EMEA recorded the lowest profit margin among all analyzed markets despite maintaining significant sales volume.

### 🇹🇷 Turkey Was the Main Source of Losses

* Turkey generated a loss of **$98,305**
* Turkey served **463 distinct customers**
* Excluding Turkey increased EMEA's profit margin from **5.46%** to **20.42%**

### 💸 Excessive Discounts Reduced Profitability

* Many subcategories received a constant **60% discount**
* High discount levels did not consistently translate into stronger sales performance or repeat purchases

### 🚚 Shipping Costs Created Additional Pressure

Analysis revealed that shipping costs varied considerably across:

* States
* Shipping modes
* Order priorities

High-priority First Class shipments generated particularly high shipping expenses, especially within:

* Tables
* Appliances
* Phones

### 📈 Scenario Modeling

A what-if analysis was conducted to evaluate alternative pricing and logistics strategies.

#### Results

| Scenario                                    |   Profit |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------: |
| Current Performance                         | -$98,305 |
| After Discount Optimization                 |  $43,753 |
| After Discount & Shipping Cost Optimization |  $44,202 |

The simulation increased EMEA's projected profit margin from **5.46%** to **20.61%**.

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce excessive discounting practices in Turkey.
2. Review First Class shipping policies for high-priority orders.
3. Optimize shipping costs for Tables, Appliances, and Phones.
4. Implement market-specific pricing strategies rather than applying uniform regional discounts.
5. Continuously monitor profitability using KPI dashboards.

---

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how data-driven analysis can be used to identify operational inefficiencies, uncover the root causes of declining profitability, and evaluate practical strategies for improving business performance.

The final recommendations transformed a significant regional loss into a projected positive profit outcome while substantially improving overall profit margins.
